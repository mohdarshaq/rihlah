FAQ

Questions, Answered

Who can join RIHLAH?

Children, adults and complete beginners are all welcome, at any level.

Do you offer Quran classes for children?

Yes. We offer patient, child-friendly classes including Quran reading, Tajweed and Short Surah Hifz.

Are classes one-to-one or group based?

Both are available. Choose one-to-one for personal focus, or small groups for interactive learning.

Do you teach Tajweed?

Yes. Tajweed is taught step by step to build correct pronunciation and confident recitation.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. We begin from the first letter and move at your pace.

How are online classes conducted?

Classes are live and online, so you can learn from home with your teacher, wherever you are in the world.

What languages are available?

Please contact us on WhatsApp or email and we will confirm the languages available for your class.

Is the first trial class free?

Yes. Your first trial class is free, with no pressure to continue.
